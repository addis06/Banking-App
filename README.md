# Banking-App

🌱 MoneyRoots – AI-Powered Savings Coach for New Savers
Seed is a cross-platform financial wellness tool designed to help new and underrepresented savers grow their money confidently. By combining mood-based AI coaching, short educational video content, and gamified savings goals, Seed makes saving simple, emotional, and rewarding.

🚀 Why MoneyRoots?
Many traditional banking apps assume users already understand money management. But for first-time savers — students, first-gen earners, and low-income users — this creates friction, stress, and dropout.

Seed was created to fill that gap.
It’s not just a savings tracker. It’s your coach, classroom, and support system.

🧩 Features
🧠 AI-Powered Budget Coach (SeedBot)
Daily check-ins with your mood

AI gives supportive budget tips:

“Feeling anxious? Save $5 and skip delivery today — you’re 20% closer to your emergency fund!”

🎓 Micro-Learning Lessons
Short, beginner-friendly video lessons on:

Budgeting 101

Building an emergency fund

Understanding credit

🎯 Savings Goals with Rewards
Set small, realistic goals (e.g., save $100 in 30 days)

Track progress visually

Unlock badges or real-life rewards
